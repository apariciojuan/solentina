## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a sanctuary of calm, conscious movement, and holistic wellness inspired by the golden sunsets of Benalmádena and the warmth of the Costa del Sol. It is tailored for wellness seekers, yoga practitioners, and individuals seeking physical balance and mental stillness in an inviting, premium space.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile Mediterranean Elegance**. It avoids the sterile, cold clinical blues and stark monochrome palettes typical of modern SaaS and health apps, instead embracing sun-drenched earth tones, airy composition, and unhurried editorial pacing. Every interaction should evoke the sensation of unrolling a mat onto warm limestone bathed in late-afternoon golden light: serene, grounded, luxurious, and restorative.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects natural elements of the Mediterranean coast: terracotta clay, sun-bleached sandstone, espresso earth, and luminous amber sunlight.

- **Primary (`#C88B52` - Terracotta Ochre)**: Used for key calls-to-action, active indicators, and prominent interactive accents. It radiates sun-kissed energy without harshness.
- **Secondary (`#A45A38` - Deep Terracotta)**: Used for emphasis, hover states, and grounding visual moments where deeper tonal contrast is required.
- **Tertiary (`#DDA15E` - Golden Sand)**: Used for subtle badge highlights, selected tag chips, soft gradient sweeps, and delicate ornamental dividers.
- **Neutral Deep (`#2D221E` - Earth Espresso)**: The primary text and iconography color. Replaces harsh `#000000` with an organic, dense, warm roast tone that ensures optimal readability against warm surfaces.
- **Neutral Light (`#FAF6F0` - Sand Cream)**: Base canvas background tone, paired with secondary container fills in `#F5EDE4` (Alabaster Linen) to create soft tonal layering without artificial borders.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances poetic, editorial poise with modern functional clarity:

- **Playfair Display** commands all hero messaging, major headings, and philosophical highlights. Its high-contrast transitional letterforms convey human warmth, heritage, and mindfulness.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a warm, humanist body framework with open apertures and balanced proportions, delivering effortless legibility for class schedules, instructor bios, and booking flows.
- **Editorial Sub-headers & Overlines**: Uppercase metadata labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) use generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.16em`) to establish sophisticated rhythm and calm spatial presence without adding weight.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid anchors desktop experiences, adapting to an 8-column layout on tablet devices and a 4-column structure on mobile.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: Max content container of `1200px` centered with variable side margins. Column gutters are set to `2rem` (`32px`), facilitating expansive breathing room between schedule cards and editorial imagery.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: Fluid 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and horizontal margins of `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: Fluid 4-column grid with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` outer edge safety margins.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace**: Generous vertical spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl` between sections) reinforces an unhurried, peaceful experience, discouraging compact or visually dense stacking.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through warm tonal layering and soft ambient diffuse shadows rather than sharp boundaries:

- **Surface Tonal Stacking**: Depth is primarily articulated by positioning cards (`#FFFFFF` or `#F5EDE4`) atop the natural background (`#FAF6F0`). This creates gentle contrast without requiring visual heaviness.
- **Sun-Kissed Diffused Shadows**: When elevation is required (such as sticky booking navigation, floating modal filters, or hovering class schedule cards), shadows incorporate an espresso-ochre tint:
  - *Low Elevation*: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 34, 30, 0.05)`
  - *Medium Elevation*: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(45, 34, 30, 0.08)`
  - *Floating Elevation*: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(45, 34, 30, 0.12)`
- **Low-Contrast Boundaries**: Outlines and dividers are soft hairline strokes (`1px solid rgba(200, 139, 82, 0.18)` or `rgba(45, 34, 30, 0.08)`), preserving an airy, tranquil structure.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts smooth organic curves (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the circular symmetry of the sun, meditation postures, and natural Mediterranean archways.

- Standard UI cards, input fields, and panels utilize a base radius of `1rem` (`16px`).
- Interactive micro-elements like chips, tags, and small badges feature fully rounded pill geometries (`9999px`) to invite gentle touch.
- Featured highlight imagery and hero banners leverage softened arches or large `1.5rem` (`24px`) rounding to echo Mediterranean sanctuary architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Ochre `#C88B52` background with `#FAF6F0` text, subtle pill radius (`9999px`), and gentle warm glow on hover (`#A45A38`). Padding: `12px 28px`.
- **Secondary / Outlined**: Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#C88B52`, `#2D221E` text, transitioning on hover to a subtle cream tint (`rgba(200, 139, 82, 0.08)`).
- **Text Link**: Earth Espresso `#2D221E` with an understated Ochre underline that expands smoothly on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips (e.g., "Full", "Few Spots", "Beginner Friendly")**: Rounded pill shape, `rgba(200, 139, 82, 0.12)` background, `#A45A38` typography in `label-sm`.
- **Filter Tags**: White `#FFFFFF` or Light Sand `#F5EDE4` background with `1px` border in `rgba(45, 34, 30, 0.08)`. Selected state fills with `#C88B52` and `#FAF6F0` text.

### Cards (Class & Event Cards)
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F5EDE4`, `16px` border radius, ambient warm shadow, padding `24px`.
- Card headers pair an overline category (`label-sm`, uppercase) with an editorial serif title (`headline-sm` in Playfair Display).
- Time, instructor, and spot availability sit within a bottom utility bar bordered by a delicate `1px` hairline divider.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form inputs feature soft cream fills (`#FFFFFF` against `#FAF6F0` backgrounds), `1px` border in `rgba(45, 34, 30, 0.15)`, and `12px` border radius.
- On focus, input borders transition to `#C88B52` with a diffuse `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 139, 82, 0.18)` glow.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons use organic rounded profiles, filling with `#C88B52` and showing a crisp warm-white check mark.

### Schedule Timeline & Calendars
- Weekly day pickers styled as soft elevated capsules with day abbreviations (`label-sm`) stacked above numerical dates (`title-md`).
- Current/selected day highlighted in `#C88B52` with `#FAF6F0` text and a subtle warm drop shadow.
